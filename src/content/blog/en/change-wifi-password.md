Changing the WiFi password on a Huawei modem is easy — just log in to the admin page at `192.168.8.1`. These steps work on the B310, B312, Orbit Star 2 (B312-929), B525, and other Huawei modems.

## Steps

1. Connect your phone to the modem's WiFi and open `192.168.8.1` in a browser.
2. Log in. If you've never changed it, use the default `admin` / `admin` — see the [default password list](https://hm.cakson.my.id/en/blog/login-192-168-8-1).
3. Open **WLAN** or **WiFi** → **WiFi Basic Settings**.
4. In the **SSID** field, rename the network if you like.
5. In the **WPA PreSharedKey** field, enter a new password — at least 8 characters.
6. Click **Save** or **Apply**.

Once saved, every connected device drops off. Reconnect them with the new password.

## Tips

- **Use a strong password** — a mix of letters, numbers and symbols. Avoid birthdays and house numbers.
- **Don't use WEP** — it's obsolete and easy to crack. Stick with WPA2/WPA3.
- **Hide your SSID?** Not recommended. The signal is still detectable and you'll only make it harder to connect new devices.
- **Frequent guests?** Enable **Guest WiFi** in Huawei Manager — a second SSID with a configurable time limit.
