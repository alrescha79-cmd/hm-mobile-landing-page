# Important Notice

## 🔧 Login Failed or Data Not Loading?

If the app **cannot retrieve data** (SMS, band switching, IP switching, etc.) or **login fails** (login goes straight to the web view without data), please follow these steps:

---

## Solution

### 1. Logout or Clear App Data

- **Logout** from the app first, or
- **Clear app data** via Phone Settings → Apps → Huawei Manager → Clear Data

### 2. Create a New Profile

After logout / clearing data:

1. Open the app → go to the **Login page**
2. Tap the **"Add Profile"** button
3. **Enter your modem name**
4. Tap **Save**

### 3. Login Again

- Log in as usual using the newly created profile
- Make sure your phone is connected to the modem's **WiFi network**

---

## ⚠️ Important

- Always ensure your phone is connected to the modem's **WiFi** before logging in
- If the issue persists after the steps above, try **restarting the modem** first
- Check if your modem firmware is up to date
